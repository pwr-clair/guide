Daily Living
Living here 🏠
🔑
Your own private home, by contract
Your stay is a short-term house rental under contract — for its whole duration the room is entirely your own space. No outside staff comes in for routine housekeeping, which also means your valuables and personal belongings are safe to leave in the room, exactly as you left them.
⏰
Freedom to live on your own clock
If your schedule turns nights into days, nothing here will wake you — there is no morning housekeeping knock. Every room has blackout curtains — so whenever you turn in, a comfortable sleep is waiting.

Laundry 🧺

🧺 How to use the washing machine
  1. Press the power button to turn on the machine.
  2. Load your laundry and add the detergent.
  3. Close the washing machine door.
  4. Select the water level, washing method and cycle according to the amount of laundry.
  5. Press Start — that's it!
🧴

TV — Netflix & YouTube 📺

📺 You can enjoy Netflix and YouTube on the TV — just press the Netflix or YouTube button on the lower part of the remote.
YOUR REMOTE — ① SET-TOP POWER BUTTON
📺 If the TV acts up
  1. Check if the set-top box under the TV shows a red light — if so, press the 'Set-top Power' button (① in the photo) to turn it blue.
  2. Check if the small connector device behind the TV shows a red light — if so, please unplug it and plug it back in until it turns blue.
  3. Still not working? Please message us — we'll sort it out right away.

Taking out the trash 🗑️

Sorting is simple — containers in your room, one drop-off spot downstairs.

🗑️
General waste
Use the white bags in the shoe cabinet by the entrance.
🥕 Food waste goes in too: drain the liquid as much as you can and seal it in a plastic bag before putting it in the general-waste bag — it keeps smells and leaks away, especially in this heat.
♻️
Recyclables
Bottles, cans, plastics and paper go in the white basket by the door.
📍
Where to drop it off
Go out through the back door of the building — you'll see the disposal area on your right. Drop everything there whenever the bag or basket is full.
