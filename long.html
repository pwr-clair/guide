Daily Living
Living here 🏠

A long stay with us works like renting a house of your own — not like staying at a hotel.

🔑
A house rental, by contract
Your stay is a short-term house rental under your contract — for its whole duration, the room is your own private home.
🚪
Complete privacy
Just like at home, no one enters your room during your stay — there are no daily housekeeping visits, so your space stays exactly as you leave it.

Heating & Cooling
Fan-coil system — the control panel is next to the bathroom door.
AIRCON PANEL — THE SETTING SHOWN = MAXIMUM COOLING
❄️ As you may have noticed, Korea is currently experiencing an unprecedented heatwave, with record-breaking temperatures reported daily. Our rooms have large windows that let in plenty of sunlight, so room temperatures can rise quickly during the daytime. Two warm recommendations: keep the air conditioning on even while you're out — rather than switching it off — and keep the blackout curtains drawn during midday hours. A fan is also provided — set it to oscillate near the aircon and the room gets much cooler. The photo above shows the panel at maximum cooling (COOL · 20.0°C · fan at max). Please stay cool and safe!

Laundry 🧺

🧺 How to use the washing machine
  1. Press the power button to turn on the machine.
  2. Load your laundry and add the detergent.
  3. Close the washing machine door.
  4. Select the water level, washing method and cycle according to the amount of laundry.
  5. Press Start — that's it!
🧴
Detergent & softener
At the bottom of the closet right in front of the bathroom door.
🧺
Drying rack
Tucked away next to the refrigerator — after use, please fold it neatly and slide it back!

TV — Netflix & YouTube 📺

📺 You can enjoy Netflix and YouTube on the TV. Press the Home (🏠) button in the center of the remote to open the left menu bar, then use the arrows around 'OK' to find the Netflix icon. For YouTube, go to the 'TV Apps' tab.
YOUR REMOTE — ① SET-TOP POWER BUTTON
📺 If the TV acts up
  1. Check if the set-top box under the TV shows a red light — if so, press the 'Set-top Power' button (① in the photo) to turn it blue.
  2. Check if the small connector device behind the TV shows a red light — if so, unplug it and plug it back in until it turns blue.
  3. Still not working? Message us — we'll sort it out!
📷 If your remote doesn't match the photo, take a picture and send it to us.

Taking out the trash 🗑️

Sorting is simple — containers in your room, one drop-off spot downstairs.

🗑️
General waste
Use the white bags in the shoe cabinet by the entrance.
🥕 Food waste goes in too: drain the liquid as much as you can and seal it in a plastic bag before putting it in the general-waste bag — it keeps smells and leaks away, especially in this heat.
♻️
Recyclables
Bottles, cans, plastics and paper go in the white basket by the door.
📍
Where to drop it off
Go out through the back door of the building — you'll see the disposal area on your right. Drop everything there whenever the bag or basket is full.
