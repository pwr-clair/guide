Long-Term Stay
Your own home, for a while 🏠

A long stay with us works like renting a house of your own — not like staying at a hotel.

🔑
A house rental, by contract
Your stay is a short-term house rental under your booking contract — for its whole duration, the room is your own private home.
🚪
Complete privacy
Just like at home, no one enters your room during your stay — there are no daily housekeeping visits, so your space stays exactly as you leave it.
🧹
Living like at home
Day-to-day tidying is in your hands, the way a home is. Before you move in, we prepare the room fully cleaned with fresh linens — from then on, it's all yours.
Waste & Recycling
Taking out the trash 🗑️

Sorting is simple — two containers in your room, one drop-off spot downstairs.

🗑️
General waste
Use the white bags in the shoe cabinet by the entrance.
♻️
Recyclables
Bottles, cans, plastics and paper go in the white basket by the door.
🥕
Food waste
Use the bin under the kitchen sink.
📍
Where to drop it off
Go out through the back door of the building — you'll see the disposal area on your right. Drop everything there whenever the bag or basket is full.
Getting Around
To Paradise City 🎰

The Paradise City resort & casino is practically next door — an easy walk, a free shuttle, or a 5-minute taxi.

ON FOOT PWR → Paradise City · ~10 min
  1. Exit the main entrance and cross the crosswalk toward the Hyatt hotel — the same crosswalk you used on arrival, in reverse.
  2. Walk past the Hyatt following the main road — the large Paradise City complex is straight ahead, you can't miss it.
  3. About 10 minutes door to door.
